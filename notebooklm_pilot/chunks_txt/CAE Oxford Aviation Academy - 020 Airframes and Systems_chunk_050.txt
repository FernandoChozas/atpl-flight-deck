Pressurization systems must be checked at periodic intervals in order to ensure that there are no serious leaks and that the pressure control components and safety devices are operating correctly. The occasions on which these tests are carried out are: • Initial proof pressure test. • When specified in the maintenance manual. • After actual or suspected system malfunction. • After repairs and modifications to the aircraft pressure hull.
